# LS Aircon Servicing Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

LS Aircon Servicing Singapore Pte. Ltd. provides air-conditioning maintenance, chemical cleaning, repairs, installation, and relocation for homes and businesses in Singapore. The proposed homepage helps visitors compare packages, identify the right service, and request a quotation.

## Main Improvements

1. **Clarify package prices and inclusions.** The published rates for six or more units need to be reconciled with the standard packages. The proposed pricing section would distinguish general servicing, chemical wash, and chemical overhaul, show what each package includes, and state whether transport fees, GST, or other charges apply. Each category would include a WhatsApp quotation link once the official number is confirmed.
2. **Help visitors find the right service.** Six prominent service categories and a guide to common problems such as leaks, weak cooling, and unusual noises would make the options easier to scan. Separate residential and commercial paths would lead to the relevant enquiry form or WhatsApp message.
3. **Make credibility and contact details easier to verify.** Genuine technician and completed-job photos, verified customer reviews, and substantiated credentials would give customers more information before booking. The homepage would explain any confirmed warranty or workmanship terms and display verified contact details and the operating address.

## Design Improvements

Consistent service cards and pricing layouts would make packages easier to compare. Placing enquiry actions beside relevant services and prices would give visitors a clear next step.
